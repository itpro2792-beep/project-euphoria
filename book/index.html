The Book of Euphoria

The prior edition is preserved in the project record, but it is not being presented here as guidance.

Why the text is held

The prior Book was an AI-generated creative simulation using role-based personas. It was not authored or reviewed by the named people, scientists, clinicians, or other experts it invoked, and no named person, tradition, Indigenous community, or other community endorsed it.

It also contained unsourced health, psychological, educational, scientific, cultural, and public-policy claims. A footer disclaimer could not make those claims reliable or safe. The public reading text is therefore withheld while the project determines what can be sourced, responsibly attributed, independently reviewed, or removed.

What this archive is not

  • Not medical, mental-health, recovery, crisis, or safety guidance.
  • Not evidence about how suffering, resilience, trauma, childhood, or healing works.
  • Not testimony from a named person, faith, culture, or community.
  • Not a consensus of human experts.

Gate for any reviewed edition

  • Every factual claim receives a source and a stated evidence class.
  • Simulated voices remain unmistakably fictional and are not quoted as authorities.
  • Cultural and Indigenous material receives appropriate community-informed review.
  • Health-adjacent claims are removed or pass qualified review within scope.

The route stays available so links do not break and the correction remains visible. The ten-minute summary is held under the same gate. For current, bounded material, use the Toolbox, published Questions, or the Status & Evidence record.

Creative archive under review. Withholding unsupported material is part of the evidence record, not an erasure of provenance.
