The Book in Ten Minutes

Shorter is not automatically safer or better sourced. The prior summary is held with the Book it condensed.

Why the summary is not displayed

The summary distilled an AI-generated role simulation into claims about suffering, trauma, resilience, childhood, culture, faith, science, public policy, and healing. It did not add sources or independent review. Its council labels could still be mistaken for the positions of real traditions or communities.

It also presented lines from the simulation as “verbatim” quotations. They may have been exact excerpts from generated text, but they were not testimony from the people, traditions, scientists, clinicians, or communities named by the simulated roles.

What is preserved

The repository history preserves the generated artifact and the correction record. Withholding it from the reading route does not pretend it never existed.

Gate for a future short edition

  • Trace every factual claim to a suitable source and evidence class.
  • Keep simulated perspectives unmistakably fictional.
  • Do not speak for cultures, faiths, communities, or experts.
  • Remove health-adjacent guidance or obtain qualified review within scope.
  • Use an editor or reviewer distinct from the generating system.
