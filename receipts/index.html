How to read this page

Build receipts are not outcome evidence

A repository commit can establish that source changed on a date. A live-page check can establish that a named build was served. Neither proves that a practice helps people, that a service works for customers, or that a financial claim is audited. Each item below names what its evidence does—and does not—establish.

Corrections are receipts too

The Book and its short summary are held

The first Book and ten-minute summary condensed an AI role simulation into cultural, scientific, health-adjacent, and public-policy claims without sufficient sourcing or independent review. Calling simulated lines “verbatim” did not turn them into testimony from the traditions or experts named by the personas. The 0.3 candidate keeps the correction visible and withholds the reading text pending review.
